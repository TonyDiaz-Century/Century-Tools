📋 Applicable NEC Code Sections
705.12(B)
Load Side Connection. The sum of all overcurrent devices (utility + inverter) connected to a busbar shall not exceed 120% of the busbar rating. Critical load panel must be sized accordingly.
702.7
Optional Standby System — Load Calculations. The calculated load of the standby system shall not exceed the capacity of the power source. No demand factor permitted when sizing inverter-backed circuits — use 100% of nameplate.
215.2(A)(1)
Feeder Ampacity. Feeders supplying continuous loads shall be sized at 125% of the continuous load current. Feeder from inverter to critical load panel: 47.5A × 125% = 59.4A → use 60A breaker / #6 AWG Cu.
230.79(C)
Service Disconnect Rating. For dwellings with loads exceeding 10 kVA, minimum 100A service. Critical load panel disconnect must be rated ≥ inverter output capacity.
220.83(B)
Existing Dwelling Load Calc. When sizing the main service panel load calc to confirm the critical load panel feeder, use 220.83(B). However, for the inverter-backed panel itself, NO demand factors apply — the inverter cannot absorb overload spikes the way a utility transformer can. Use 100% of all simultaneous loads.
